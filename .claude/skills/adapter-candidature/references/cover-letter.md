
## Cover letter

- Keep it to one A4 page and ~250 words (for the body) unless the user asks for more.
- Use a professional but natural voice. Avoid stock openings, flattery and inflated claims.
- Mention the exact role and its reference. Connect the employer's needs to two or three concrete pieces of evidence from the profile.
- Acknowledge a meaningful gap briefly when necessary. Then show transferable evidence and a realistic learning path.
- End with availability for an interview and an appropriate salutation.
