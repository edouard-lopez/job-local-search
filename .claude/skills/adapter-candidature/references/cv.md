
## CV

- Aim for two A4 pages for an experienced profile. Use one page when I ask it.
- Keep the reading order clear for humans and applicant-tracking systems (ATS, the software that scans CVs). Use real text, semantic headings and conventional section names. Never encode critical information only in icons or graphics.
- Start with name, target title, contact details and relevant links. Follow with a compact profile and a scannable skills section.
- Order experience by relevance, and keep dates clear. Each important bullet combines an action, a scope or method, and an outcome when the profile supports one.
- Label each entry accurately: professional work, independent work, open-source work, teaching, volunteering or personal project.
- Avoid self-ratings, decorative skill bars, vague adjectives and unsupported "expert" claims.
- Keep body text at a readable print size, typically 10 pt or more.
